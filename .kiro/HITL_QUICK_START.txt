YOUR COMMAND (Perfect! ✅):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
fathom run "Ask GPT to do deep research about opencrawler(moltybot)" \
  --use-xml --serial emulator-5554 --verbose --interactive

Or shorter:
fathom run "Ask GPT to do deep research about opencrawler(moltybot)" \
  --use-xml -s emulator-5554 -v -i
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


HOW IT WORKS:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Agent starts executing your intent
2. When agent is uncertain (confidence < 50%), it AUTOMATICALLY asks you:

   ❓ Agent Question
   ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ The agent is uncertain (confidence: 45%) about what to do next │
   │                                                                 │
   │ Current screen: com.android.launcher                           │
   │ Intent: Ask GPT to do deep research about opencrawler          │
   │ Suggested action: Tap on Element at [540, 960]                │
   │                                                                 │
   │ What should the agent do?                                      │
   └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
   Your answer: _

3. You type your guidance and press Enter
4. Agent uses your guidance to make better decisions
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


WHAT TO TYPE:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
✅ GOOD Examples:

Your answer: Open the ChatGPT app (green icon with white logo)

Your answer: Tap the search bar at the top and type "opencrawler"

Your answer: First login with test@example.com, then navigate to search

Your answer: Scroll down to find the settings menu

Your answer: The button you want is at the bottom right corner


❌ AVOID:

Your answer: Just do it
Your answer: I don't know
Your answer: Click something
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


WHEN AGENT ASKS:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Option 1: Provide specific guidance
  → Type your answer and press Enter
  → Agent re-analyzes with your guidance
  → Agent makes better decision

Option 2: Let agent continue
  → Just press Enter (no text)
  → Agent continues with its suggested action
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


HOW YOUR GUIDANCE HELPS:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WITHOUT your guidance:
  LLM sees: Intent + Current Screen + Available Actions
  
WITH your guidance:
  LLM sees: Intent + Current Screen + Available Actions + YOUR GUIDANCE
  
Result: Much better decisions! 🎯

TROUBLESHOOTING:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Q: Agent never asks questions?
A: Good! It means agent is confident. Or you forgot -i flag.

Q: How do I pause manually?
A: Not implemented yet. Agent pauses automatically when uncertain.

Q: Agent ignores my guidance?
A: Make sure guidance is specific and you pressed Enter.
